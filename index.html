Executive summary

Titan Roofing of Florida has the stronger website. The three competitors have stronger reputations.

On the website itself, Titan Roofing of Florida leads on deeper commercial content and clearer warranty terms. Collis Roofing, Inc., Patrick Roofing, Inc. and DC Roofing, Inc. lead on longer operating histories, higher manufacturer tiers, bigger review counts and packaged offers. They also appear in the "best roofer in Melbourne" lists that Google and AI assistants draw on, and Titan Roofing of Florida doesn't.

What is holding Titan Roofing of Florida back

  • Reputation looks newer and thinner.
    • Patrick Roofing, Inc. has operated since 1986 and has held a BBB A+ since 1997.
    • DC Roofing, Inc. shows 4.9 stars from 256 reviews on GAF.
    • Collis Roofing, Inc. claims 32 years and Owens Corning Platinum Preferred status.
    • Titan Roofing of Florida's BBB profile (A-) dates the LLC to March 2025.
  • Missing from "best roofer" lists. Collis Roofing, Inc. published two Melbourne rankings in August and September 2026. Both name Patrick Roofing, Inc. and/or DC Roofing, Inc. Neither names Titan Roofing of Florida.
  • Its own pages compete with each other. The homepage outranks the commercial page for commercial searches. Two emergency pages split "emergency roof repair" (3,888 impressions, #21).

Where Titan Roofing of Florida already leads

  • North Brevard is open. None of the three competitors has a page for Titusville, Merritt Island, Cocoa or Mims.
  • A Brevard page that works. Titan Roofing of Florida has 1 page targeting Brevard County, the same as Collis Roofing, Inc. (its Melbourne page). Patrick Roofing, Inc. has none, and DC Roofing, Inc.'s 6 city links all return 404 errors.
  • Clearer customer terms. Titan Roofing of Florida offers a 15-year workmanship warranty, a best-price guarantee against written estimates, a 20-year GAF NDL commercial warranty and 24/7 service. Patrick Roofing, Inc. and DC Roofing, Inc. publish no warranty terms.

Competitor marketing strategies & activity

What each competitor is doing

Each competitor wins in a different way: packaged offers from a large operator, long-standing reputation, or a high volume of content.

Collis Roofing, Inc. Statewide · Melbourne branch

Strategy: Uses scale and packaged offers, and places itself at the top of "best roofer" searches.

Activity

  • Since July 2026, has published "best contractors in [city]" rankings that list itself first.
    • Melbourne commercial ranking (Aug 24): Patrick Roofing, Inc. #2, DC Roofing, Inc. #3.
    • Melbourne residential ranking (Sep 8): DC Roofing, Inc. #3.
  • Runs the Collis Club maintenance plan, a Quick Quote aerial estimate, and a $300 free inspection. Customers get 50% of a repair's cost back if they buy a new roof within 5 years.
  • Offers four lenders, including PACE financing, and a lifetime transferable workmanship warranty through Owens Corning Platinum.
  • Active on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, X and LinkedIn. Its Melbourne page runs about 2,200 words with an 8-question FAQ and a service-area map.

Weak spots

  • Only one Brevard page, with no Brevard repair or commercial page.
  • The Melbourne page shows the Longwood address, and listings use different phone numbers.
  • Melbourne's Google profile may be weak: Birdeye shows 3.9 stars from 53 reviews (unverified).
  • No Brevard commercial projects are published.

Patrick Roofing, Inc. Melbourne · reputation leader

Strategy: Relies on 40 years of reputation and word of mouth. It does almost no SEO.

Activity

  • BBB A+ since 1997, FRSA Outstanding Safety Award, 20-Year Diamond Award from the Melbourne-Palm Bay Chamber, sponsor of WFIT.
  • About 5.0 stars on Google; the website claims 130+ five-star reviews. Angi 5.0 from 50 reviews.
  • Offers 0% financing for 12 months, free wind mitigation and help with My Safe Florida Home grants.
  • Listed on Expertise.com's "Best Melbourne Roofers".

Weak spots

  • About 21 pages, no city pages, no commercial pages.
  • Most inner pages use the default title "Patrick Roofing Site" and have no meta description.
  • No sitemap, no schema, and no blog posts since 2019.
  • Its address differs across directories.

DC Roofing, Inc. Melbourne · content volume

Strategy: Publishes a high volume of blog posts on cost, metal roofing, insurance and hurricanes. Also promotes local awards and a metal-roofing specialty.

Activity

  • 95 blog posts targeting Melbourne, Palm Bay, Satellite Beach, Rockledge and Melbourne Beach.
  • GAF Certified Plus (4.9 from 256 reviews), BBB A+, "Best of Space Coast 2026", and a 9,000 sq ft in-house metal fabrication shop.
  • Financing through Renew Financial (PACE), Enhancify and Launch Credit Union.
  • Community PR: a Space Coast Daily feature, a Space Coast Symposium sponsorship at the Astronaut Memorial Foundation, and donations to Brevard Public Schools.

Weak spots

  • Moved from dcroofingbrevard.com to dcroofingflorida.com using 302 redirects that all point to the homepage.
  • All six city links return 404 errors.
  • No stated warranty and an empty testimonials page.
  • Conflicting claims about how long it has been in business.
  • Only 2 commercial pages.

Where competitors are outperforming Titan Roofing of Florida

Six areas where competitors are ahead, most severe first

Each gap below is an area where at least one benchmark competitor is visibly outperforming Titan Roofing of Florida, with the competitor named in the heading.

Critical

Reputation and length of operation (Patrick Roofing, Inc.; Collis Roofing, Inc.; DC Roofing, Inc.)

All three show long histories or high review counts. Patrick Roofing, Inc. has 40 years and a BBB A+ since 1997. DC Roofing, Inc. shows 4.9 stars from 256 reviews on its GAF profile. Collis Roofing, Inc. claims 32 years and a list of national awards. Titan Roofing of Florida shows no overall rating on its website. Its Better Business Bureau profile (A-) dates the LLC to 2025, which conflicts with the "30+ years" claim unless the About page explains the history.

Critical

Presence in "best roofer" rankings and AI answers (Collis Roofing, Inc.)

Collis Roofing, Inc.'s Melbourne commercial ranking (Aug 24, 2026) and residential ranking (Sep 8, 2026) both list Collis Roofing, Inc. first and include Patrick Roofing, Inc. and DC Roofing, Inc. Expertise.com's Melbourne list also includes Patrick Roofing, Inc. Titan Roofing of Florida isn't on any list we found. Google and AI assistants pull from these lists when answering "who is the best roofer in Melbourne," so Titan Roofing of Florida is missing from those answers.

High

Packaged offers and ways to convert (Collis Roofing, Inc.)

Collis Roofing, Inc. offers several ways to start: Collis Club membership ($19.99 a month for residential, from $375 a year for commercial), a Quick Quote online estimate, a free $300 inspection, 50% of a repair's cost back toward a new roof, and referral payments. Titan Roofing of Florida has no maintenance plan and no online estimate.

High

Manufacturer certification tier (Collis Roofing, Inc.; DC Roofing, Inc.)

Collis Roofing, Inc. is Owens Corning Platinum Preferred, the top tier, which lets it offer lifetime transferable workmanship coverage. DC Roofing, Inc. is GAF Certified Plus. Titan Roofing of Florida shows Owens Corning Preferred. Its warranty page lists a GAF 20-year NDL, but it doesn't display a GAF certification badge.

High

Blog topics homeowners search for (DC Roofing, Inc.)

DC Roofing, Inc. has 95 posts on cost, metal vs. shingle, insurance and hurricane prep, each tied to a Brevard city. AI answers draw on posts like these. Titan Roofing of Florida has about 35 posts, mostly on engineering topics. Almost every one gets fewer than 150 impressions a year.

Medium

Melbourne page depth (Collis Roofing, Inc.)

Collis Roofing, Inc.'s Melbourne page runs about 2,200 words, with 13 sections (local permits, weather, materials), 8 FAQs and a service-area map. Titan Roofing of Florida's Melbourne page is a similar length but has 4 FAQs, no zip codes and no local permit content. It averages #34.

Opportunities to counter competitors

How Titan Roofing of Florida beats each competitor

Against Collis Roofing, Inc.

  • Build Brevard depth. Collis Roofing, Inc. has one Brevard page and treats north Brevard as an afterthought. Titan Roofing of Florida can position itself as the Brevard-based roofer against a Longwood-based chain.
  • Answer the Collis Club with a "Titan Protect" plan that has residential and commercial tiers and published prices.
  • Claim county-wide commercial searches through the optimized Brevard County location page and the new commercial service pages. Collis Roofing, Inc.'s only commercial area page is for Orlando.
  • Add an online estimate request with a fast response promise to compete with Quick Quote.

Against Patrick Roofing, Inc.

  • Win on the website. Patrick Roofing, Inc. has no city pages, no commercial pages and default title tags. Titan Roofing of Florida should outrank it for anything beyond its brand name.
  • Own commercial roofing outright. Patrick Roofing, Inc. never mentions commercial work on its website.
  • Close the review gap by requesting a review after every job and showing the rating on every page. Patrick Roofing, Inc.'s near-perfect rating is its main advantage.

Against DC Roofing, Inc.

  • Act while DC Roofing, Inc.'s domain move is still unsettled. Its 302 redirects and broken city pages leave Melbourne and Palm Bay rankings open, so push the Titan Roofing of Florida Melbourne and Palm Bay pages now.
  • Lead with warranty terms. DC Roofing, Inc. publishes none, while Titan Roofing of Florida has a 15-year workmanship warranty and a price-beat guarantee.
  • Answer its content volume with fewer, better posts on the same topics (cost, metal vs. shingle, insurance), written for Titusville and Merritt Island.

Actionable insights

90-day plan to protect and grow Titan Roofing of Florida's position in Brevard

Weeks 1–2 Quick wins

  1. Tighten the Titusville commercial page. Keep /roofing/commercial-roofing/ targeting Titusville, FL. It already gets 1,020 impressions a year for "commercial roofing titusville fl". Add Titusville-specific detail (the Space Coast industrial corridor, local permitting, nearby projects) and point the homepage's commercial links at it, so the homepage stops ranking in its place.
  2. Merge the emergency pages. 301 /roofing/emergency-roofing/ into /roofing/emergency-roof-repair/, or the reverse if the first page has more backlinks.
  3. Show the reputation. Add the Google rating and review count, the BBB seal and the Owens Corning Preferred badge above the fold on the homepage, commercial page and city pages. If Titan Roofing of Florida holds GAF certification, show that badge too.
  4. Add schema and llms.txt. Add RoofingContractor and Organization schema with sameAs links to the Google Business Profile, Better Business Bureau, Facebook and Yelp. Add FAQPage schema to service pages and publish /llms.txt. None of the three competitors has an llms.txt.

Weeks 3–6 Close the biggest gaps

  1. Rebuild the Titusville page with neighborhoods, zip codes 32780 and 32796, nearby projects, 8+ FAQs and a quick-answer summary. Target: move "roofing titusville" from #23 into the top 5.
  2. Re-optimize the Brevard County location page (/areas-we-serve/brevard-county/) for roofing, including commercial.
    • Its title and H1 currently target "Exterior Construction Services in Brevard County".
    • Retitle it to "Roofing & Commercial Roofing Contractor in Brevard County, FL" and change the H1 to match.
    • Expand the existing commercial section and grow the FAQ from 4 to 8+ questions.
    • Link it to the Titusville commercial page and the new commercial service pages.
    This gives county-wide and Melbourne commercial searches a page to land on, without creating a new URL.
  3. Launch 4 commercial service pages linked from the commercial roofing page and the Brevard County page: Commercial Roof Repair, Commercial Roof Replacement, Flat Roofing, and TPO Roofing (split out from modified bitumen).
  4. Rewrite the About page to explain the team's 30+ years of experience alongside the 2025 LLC, and link Titan Construction & Consulting LLC as the sister company.
  5. Rewrite the Melbourne page to match the Collis Roofing, Inc. page's depth: local permits, zip codes, 8 FAQs and a commercial section.

Weeks 7–13 Expand the footprint

  1. Add city pages for Mims, Port St. John, Rockledge and Viera, and rewrite the Palm Bay page while DC Roofing, Inc.'s city pages are down.
  2. Launch the "Titan Protect" maintenance program page, an online estimate request, and Commercial Roof Coatings and Industrial/Warehouse Roofing pages.
  3. Change the blog mix to 4 posts a month. Two homeowner posts (cost, insurance, My Safe Florida Home, Titusville permits) and two commercial posts that link to the new commercial pages. Add bylines from the license holder.
  4. Strengthen local signals. Add all commercial services to the Google Business Profile, post weekly, and consider PACE financing to match Collis Roofing, Inc. and DC Roofing, Inc.
